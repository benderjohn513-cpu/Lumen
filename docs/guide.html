Setup guide

From install to play in a few minutes.

Lumen is the player. What you can watch comes from add-ons you choose. Here's how they work and the quickest way to set them up.

What add-ons are

Add-ons are small web services made by other people. Lumen asks them what exists (catalogs, posters, episode lists), where something can be played (streams), and which subtitles are available.

Lumen uses the same add-on format as apps like Stremio, so any guide or add-on made for those apps works in Lumen too, usually with fewer steps.

Install the starter pack

2. Add streaming add-ons

The starter pack tells Lumen what exists. To actually play things you add one or more stream add-ons. Lumen doesn't pick these for you: choose ones you trust and have the right to use.

  1. Browse inside Lumen. Open Add-ons → Discover for the official and community lists, with a short description of each.
  2. Use a community pack. The pack gallery has sets other Lumen users have shared. Their keys are always stripped out.
  3. Follow any add-on guide. Guides written for apps like Stremio work the same way here. When a guide says "click Install", see the next section.

Live sports in one step

Lumen's Sports tab plays live games and 24/7 sports channels from the Sports Streams add-on. You set it up once, right in Lumen:

Sports Streams Premium ($3 a month: dedicated servers, more games, replays)

  1. Get Premium on the Sports Streams site. Your token arrives by email.
  2. In Lumen, open Sports, paste the token in the Watch live games right here box and press Connect.

That's it. Lumen checks the token, picks the server closest to you, sets your timezone and installs it.

The free version

  1. In Lumen, open Sports and press Get the free version.
  2. On the page that opens, tick the box to show you're human and press Install. Lumen catches it.

Then press Choose sports and pick your leagues. Lumen shows those games first and tells Sports Streams to show just those sports. Click any live game and it plays.

Installing a pack? Both of Arthur Morgan's packs include Sports Streams: paste your Premium token in the pack window, or leave it empty for the free version.

Install links open in Lumen

Many add-on websites have a Configure page with an Install button. Lumen can catch those buttons:

  1. In Lumen, open Settings and turn on Open add-on install links in Lumen.
  2. On the add-on's website, set it up and click Install.
  3. Lumen opens and asks "Install this add-on?", showing its name and what it adds. Click Install.

Using another app with the same add-ons? Your computer lets only one app open these links, so leave the switch off if you'd rather that app gets them. You can always copy the link instead: right-click Install → Copy link, then paste it in Add-ons → Paste an add-on link.

Real-Debrid (optional)

Some stream add-ons work with a paid service like Real-Debrid for faster, more reliable playback. Lumen doesn't need it, but if you have it:

  1. Copy your API key from your Real-Debrid account page.
  2. In Lumen, open Add-ons → Keys & accounts and paste it once.
  3. Every add-on and pack that needs it uses that key. It stays on your device and never goes into packs you share.

Bring your add-ons from another app

Already set up in another app that uses add-ons? On a PC, open Add-ons → Bring them from another app. Lumen copies your add-ons, library and Continue Watching, and changes nothing in the other app.

Moving between your own devices? Turn on sync in Settings → Sync. It uses your own Google Drive, so there's no Lumen account.

Tips

  • Search anywhere with Ctrl + K on a PC.
  • Torrent-based sources need the free Stremio Service running on your PC or network. Point Lumen at it in Settings → Playback → Streaming server.
  • Turn on Spoiler Shield in Settings if you're catching up on something.
  • Share your setup with friends: Add-ons → Packs → Share your add-ons. Your keys are removed automatically.
