Share your own

In Lumen open Add-ons → Packs → Share your add-ons (on phones: More → Add-ons → Share your add-ons), make your pack and press Upload to the pack gallery. It opens GitHub with everything filled in; post it and it appears here once it's approved. Lumen doesn't make, host or check third-party add-ons.
