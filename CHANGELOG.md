# What's new in Lumen

Lumen also shows these notes inside the app, with screenshots, short clips and **Try it** buttons that take you straight to each feature.

## November 21, 2026 — Windows 3.0.0 · Android 1.3.0

**New**
- **Add-on packs** — one link installs a whole set of add-ons. Share yours from *Add-ons → Packs*; your keys and accounts are stripped out automatically.
- **Watch party on phones and TVs** — join or host a party with PCs, phones and TVs; play, pause, skipping and the episode stay in sync.
- **The story bar** — Story Bridge draws the whole story on one line: each anime season as a stretch of chapters, then the manga past it, with a marker where you are.
- **Colours from artwork** — title pages take on the colours of their own artwork.
- **Welcome tour** — four slides on what makes Lumen different. Open it any time from Settings / More.
- **Story Bridge stays current** — once a month Lumen re-checks running manga and recent anime, so new seasons show up without an app update.
- **Update check** — Lumen tells you when a new version is out on GitHub.
- **Android TV downloads** and **kids profiles** that only show family-friendly titles.

## November 20, 2026

- **Story Bridge** — where the anime stops in the manga, with a Read button that opens that chapter. Over 1,000 series built in, plus live-action adaptations (Alice in Borderland, One Piece and hundreds more).
- **Anime or manga?** — when fans clearly prefer one, Lumen says so (Tokyo Ghoul: read the manga; Your Lie in April: watch the anime).
- **Quiet anime ↔ manga sync** — finish an episode and the manga's Continue button moves to the matching chapter. It only moves forward, so rewatching never loses your place.
- **Spoiler Shield, everywhere** — one switch hides episodes, scores and chapter titles past where you are.
- **Smart Skip** — skip filler, recaps and (optionally) the weakest episodes of long shows.
- **Time-fit** — "I have 45 minutes": what you can finish in that time, plus a binge planner.
- **Picture quality you can see** — the player shows what Lumen is doing to the picture, and Compare shows the original for a few seconds.
- **Sports catch-up** — join a game late and see every scoring play, lead changes and the biggest lead.
- **A tidier Lumen** — rarely used options fold away; nothing was removed.
