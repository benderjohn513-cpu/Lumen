# What's new in Lumen

Lumen also shows these notes inside the app, with screenshots, short clips and **Try it** buttons that take you straight to each feature.

## November 27, 2026 — Lumen 3.1.0

**New**
- **Update pop-up** — when a new Lumen is out, a pop-up says **Update available** with what's new. **Update now** downloads it and restarts Lumen on Windows, or opens the installer on Android; on Linux it saves the new version to your Downloads. **Later** asks again next time.
- **Search for "good" ones** — "good horror movies", "great sci-fi shows", "amazing anime": words like good, great and amazing pick well-rated, well-known titles instead of titles with that word in the name. "The Good Place" still finds The Good Place.
- **Search by what it's about** — "a movie about a shark", "a show about a chemistry teacher who makes drugs", "movies about chess". Lumen reads plot summaries and subjects, and knows similar words (shark → great white, AI → robot).
- **See why it matched** — every Smart Search result shows what matched your words.
- **Refine a search** (computers) — type more under a Smart Search to narrow it ("under two hours", "less action", "exclude watched"); each change is a chip you can remove. "Sad and funny" now needs both, and whole sentences work: "could you show me a funny mystery".

Windows, Android and Linux are 3.1.0. Mac stays on the 3.0.0 beta for now.

## November 24, 2026

**New**
- **Live sports in one step** — the Sports tab now sets up **Sports Streams** for you. Paste your Premium token and Lumen checks it, picks the server closest to you, sets your timezone and installs it. No token? Press **Get the free version**, tick “I'm human” on the page that opens, press Install, and Lumen catches it. Then choose your sports: Lumen shows those games and tells Sports Streams to show just those sports. Click any live game and it plays (computers and Android).
- **Both of Arthur Morgan's packs include live sports** — paste your Sports Streams Premium token in the pack window, or leave it empty for the free version.

## November 23, 2026

**New**
- **Every version of a title** — when sources have more than one version, pick it under Play or switch in the player and keep your place: black & white or colour, theatrical, extended or director's cut, IMAX or widescreen, and the original 4:3 for shows. A show's pick covers every episode.
- **Episode orders** — The Melancholy of Haruhi Suzumiya in broadcast or chronological order, Kaleidoscope in story order or shuffled (always starting with Black and ending with White), and anthologies like Love, Death & Robots shuffled. Next episode follows the order you picked.
- **Story order for anime franchises** — Steins;Gate (including 23β and Steins;Gate 0 in the right spot) and Kara no Kyoukai in the order the story happens (computer apps).
- **Turn sections off** — don't watch anime, or never use Sports? Switch a whole section off (Movies, Shows, Anime, Read, Sports, My List, and YouTube and Twitch on computers) and its tab and titles disappear everywhere: Home, search, Continue Watching and recommendations. Switch it back on any time in *Settings → Appearance → Sections* (computers) or *More → Sections & rows* (Android).
- **Steins;Gate: Definitive Edition** — a title only on Lumen: the whole story in one run. Episodes 1–22, then 23β into all of Steins;Gate 0, then back for the true ending (23–24), the Egoistic Poriomania OVA and finally the movie, Load Region of Déjà Vu. Switch to *Original release* order any time, or jump to the original Steins;Gate and Steins;Gate 0 from the title page. Search "Steins;Gate" to find it (computers and Android).
- **Commentary tracks stay off** — Lumen never picks a commentary audio track for you; it's still there in the audio menu (computer apps).

## November 22, 2026

**New**
- **Smart Search** — describe what you're in the mood for ("comfort anime", "korean revenge thriller", "90s sitcom", "shows like The Office") and Lumen finds it, free and with no AI key. Titles that match everything you asked for come first, then close matches, and each result says why it was picked. Recent searches are one tap away.
- **Cleaner search** — each title once, with its movies and the canon prequel next to it, and everything else tucked under Extras. Nicknames work too ("jjk", "csm").
- **Install links from add-on websites** — turn on *Open add-on install links in Lumen* in Settings, and the Install button on an add-on's website opens Lumen and asks before adding it.
- **Lumen Starter pack** — the official beginner pack (catalogs and subtitles) is pinned at the top of the pack gallery.

## November 21, 2026 — Windows 3.0.0 · Android 1.3.0

**New**
- **Add-on packs** — one link installs a whole set of add-ons. Share yours from *Add-ons → Packs*; your keys and accounts are stripped out automatically.
- **Watch party on phones and TVs** — join or host a party with PCs, phones and TVs; play, pause, skipping and the episode stay in sync.
- **The story bar** — Story Bridge draws the whole story on one line: each anime season as a stretch of chapters, then the manga past it, with a marker where you are.
- **Colours from artwork** — title pages take on the colours of their own artwork.
- **Welcome tour** — four slides on what makes Lumen different. Open it any time from Settings / More.
- **Story Bridge stays current** — once a month Lumen re-checks running manga and recent anime, so new seasons show up without an app update.
- **Update check** — Lumen tells you when a new version is out on GitHub.
- **Android TV downloads** and **kids profiles** that only show family-friendly titles.

## November 20, 2026

- **Story Bridge** — where the anime stops in the manga, with a Read button that opens that chapter. Over 1,000 series built in, plus live-action adaptations (Alice in Borderland, One Piece and hundreds more).
- **Anime or manga?** — when fans clearly prefer one, Lumen says so (Tokyo Ghoul: read the manga; Your Lie in April: watch the anime).
- **Quiet anime ↔ manga sync** — finish an episode and the manga's Continue button moves to the matching chapter. It only moves forward, so rewatching never loses your place.
- **Spoiler Shield, everywhere** — one switch hides episodes, scores and chapter titles past where you are.
- **Smart Skip** — skip filler, recaps and (optionally) the weakest episodes of long shows.
- **Time-fit** — "I have 45 minutes": what you can finish in that time, plus a binge planner.
- **Picture quality you can see** — the player shows what Lumen is doing to the picture, and Compare shows the original for a few seconds.
- **Sports catch-up** — join a game late and see every scoring play, lead changes and the biggest lead.
- **A tidier Lumen** — rarely used options fold away; nothing was removed.
